Our Mission

Offering Pet Families Honor, Dignity, and Respectful Services

For many who are visiting, the loss of your beloved pet is either very recent, or will be happening in the near future. On behalf of everyone at Resting Rainbow, please accept our deepest sympathies. We hope that all of the following resources are helpful to you as you navigate through your feelings of grief and loss.

Our Services

Welcome to the Resting Rainbow!

WE PROVIDE A FULL LINE OF SERVICES AND PRODUCTS

We offer an affordable pricing and unparalleled services with the best tracking and record keeping system available.

CERTIFICATE OF CREMATION

You will receive a Certificate of Cremation signed by one of our staff that also expresses our sympathy to you and your family.

24 TO 48 HOUR TURN-AROUND

We can offer a 24 to 48 hour turn-around to provide closure to your loss.

FUNERAL HOME QUALITY

The equipment we use in the cremation process are built to meet government standards regarding emissions and air quality.

PRESERVING THE MEMORY OF YOUR PET
